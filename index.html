Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add a book?
    Tap the + button in the tab bar, search by title or ISBN, and tap the book to add it to your library.

  • What is the Weekly Wrap Card?
    At the end of each week, furtherbook generates a beautiful AI-powered summary card based on your notes and reading activity.

  • How do I change the app language?
    Go to Settings → Appearance → Language. Currently supported: English and Turkish.

  • How do I restore my purchase?
    Go to Settings → Plan → Restore Purchases.

  • Is my data backed up?
    Yes. Your library, notes, and reading logs are securely stored in the cloud and synced across your devices.
